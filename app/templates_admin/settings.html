Google Ads conversions export

These files feed Google Ads the real, captured margin per booking (keyed on the visitor's stored gclid), plus retractions for anything refunded or voided. Download and upload them in Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → Uploads, or set up a scheduled pull (below) so Google fetches them automatically.

Hands-off option: in Google Ads → Conversions → Uploads → Schedules, add an HTTPS source on a daily/weekly cadence pointing at your export URLs (they need the secret CONVERSIONS_EXPORT_TOKEN set as a config var):
/exports/conversions.csv?key=YOUR_TOKEN
/exports/retractions.csv?key=YOUR_TOKEN
Google then imports on its own — no manual upload. The conversion action name must match CONVERSIONS_ACTION_NAME (default “Confirmed booking”).
